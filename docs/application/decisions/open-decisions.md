## Partially resolved membership and invitation decisions

- **O09:** [ADR-003 B1/B4](003-bootstrap-groups-and-invitations.md#b1-explicit-administrative-bootstrap-o09) settles explicit administrative bootstrap, designated-identity-only group creation and atomic transfer eligibility. Transfer implementation is low-priority deferred [#84](https://github.com/haggaikaunda/kami-split/issues/84); exactly one active owner and no removal/demotion bypass remain required. [#75](https://github.com/haggaikaunda/kami-split/issues/75) retains concrete identity/membership uniqueness, ownership representation, transaction/lock protocol, safe departure, Auth-link deletion/retry, minimal household/creation-grant representation and membership/profile visibility design. Bootstrap procedure implementation is [#81](https://github.com/haggaikaunda/kami-split/issues/81), not this register.
- **O10:** [ADR-003 B5/B6](003-bootstrap-groups-and-invitations.md#b5-invitation-lifecycle-o10) settles seven-day expiry, same-link expired resend, one outstanding invitation including expired records, explicit duplicate/resend handling, terminal states, serialized race outcomes and reusable-token protection. Rejoining requires a new invitation and preserves membership identity. [#79](https://github.com/haggaikaunda/kami-split/issues/79), with #75 and [#80](https://github.com/haggaikaunda/kami-split/issues/80), retains email comparison/current-verification evidence, expiry clock/boundary, locks/admission and authorization rechecks, rejoin role/history visibility, delivery-failure semantics, key/format details and retention decisions.

These remaining choices reflect the #77 baseline. New owner-approved membership/profile or returning-member policies in #75/#79, or financial contract/recovery policies in [#76](https://github.com/haggaikaunda/kami-split/issues/76), require separately reviewed follow-up documentation; they are not #77 dependencies. Distinguish **owner-approved, reviewed documentation pending** from unanswered questions when consulting live issues. Do not infer readiness or implementation from an approval alone.
