How your balance works

We compare what you paid with your share, then include any repayments you’ve recorded.

Pay more than your share and you’re owed money. Pay less and you owe money.

See an example

You paid $60 and your share was $20. You’re owed $40. If someone repays you $15, you’re still owed $25.

Why can my total be $0?

You may have nothing left to pay or collect. Or what you owe in one group may match what you’re owed in another. Those payments still need to be settled separately.

Other members may still owe each other, even when your balance is zero.
